## Findings

### [MEDIUM] scripts/run_baseline.sh:236,251 — hardcoded 1500.0 in score formula

The baseline score formula subtracts a literal `1500.0` from `last_candidate_elo`
instead of the configurable `HYZERO_OPPONENT_INITIAL_ELO`.

**Failure scenario:** A user sets `HYZERO_OPPONENT_INITIAL_ELO=2000`. In Rust,
`candidate_elo` bootstraps to 2000, so `last_candidate_elo ≈ 2000` even at zero
real Elo progress. The awk formula `(last_candidate_elo - 1500.0) * elo_score_weight`
evaluates to `(2000-1500)*0.05 = +25.0` "score" from the initial offset alone,
masking real signal. The same mismatch is in the fallback (line ~231 sets
`LAST_CANDIDATE_ELO="1500.0"`) and in the formula (line ~251).

**Fix:** Read `HYZERO_OPPONENT_INITIAL_ELO` (default 1500) once at the top of the
script and substitute it in both the fallback and the awk expression.
